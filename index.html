Outrageous plot, many side quests; reads fast, rereads well.

A wildly entertaining account of one woman's determination to amuse herself first and ask questions later. A stubborn idealist; she really believes the best is yet to come.

Friends describe her as impossible to keep up with and even harder to say no to. She's deep into her 189th book of the year, hosts anyone who'll show up, and collects hats and stories in roughly equal number.

She keeps a standing appointment with ballet, whether trying to plié deeper or watching (gasping) professionals, and treats Brooklyn as one long walk still being mapped, block by block, neighborhood by neighborhood, in search of whatever's around the corner.

The culmination of thirty-four years spent saying yes to nearly everything, this edition is on offer to a reader who can keep pace: an extrovert, ideally, who has never once turned down a good time.
